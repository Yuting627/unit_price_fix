2 Similarity Logic:
1. Compare the target shop only against shops belonging to the same IBD
2. Fill missing weeks after the first observation with 0 to create continuous histories
3. Remove week 2407 from similarity computation
4. Apply a log transformation and calculate week-over-week change to focus on trend shape
                            
5. Compare only the weeks available in both shops and require a minimum overlap
              
6. Measure the difference between the two transformed trend series using Euclidean distance & adjust 
the distance by the number of overlapping weeks
               
7. Sort candidate shops by Normalized Distance: Lower score = higher similarity
