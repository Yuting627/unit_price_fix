At individual market level
At individual product level
For specific facts


“Trend check” is a new period validation algorithm which is assessing if this data is in line with back period data
Consistent in terms of level
Reflect seasonal behavior
Reflect effect of irregular events 


Markets*Items*Facts*Periods with data not consistent with back periods  are flagged as exceptions\n\n===== SLIDE 3 =====\nTrend check approach\nBuild time series model by Market*Product*Fact.\n
Predict new period data and establish Lower and Upper bounds of tolerance.
\nValidate actual new period  data against tolerance bounds.
\n\n===== SLIDE 4 =====\nTime series model\n\n===== SLIDE 5 =====\nData/Inputs\nIrregular event calendar
It covers:
Events/holidays that are present every year but at different time (e.g. Easter, Ramadan, etc.)
Big events that are happening once (e.g. Olympic games, Football world cup, etc.)
Event calendar is transformed into dichotomous indicator variables (one variable per event), showing if given event is present in specific period (1) or not (0) 


Data of given fact for specific product in specific market in historical periods
Data of non-additive facts or additive facts for Total country and Total category is used as is
Data of additive facts for more granular MBDs or products is first transformed into relative form by dividing it by Total country*Total category value in a given period (i.e. calculating contribution or share). This is used to perform validation\n\n===== SLIDE 6 =====\nconcept\nExponential smoothing (Holt Winters)\nRegression method\nModel combines 2 techniques\n\n===== SLIDE 7 =====\nConcept\nAdditive combination of both methods\nNote: if historical periods contain less than 2 years of data regression part and seasonal components are not used - set to 0\n\n===== SLIDE 8 =====\nModeling Process\n\n===== SLIDE 9 =====\nRegression part\nRegression model is constructed separately from Exponential smoothing
